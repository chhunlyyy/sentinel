Fix payroll rounding · specification v1

sha256 4f1c09ab…e21d

Problem

Net pay is rounded per line item, so a payslip with many allowances can be off by up to 0.04 against the bank file.

Requirements

  1. REQ-1 Round once, on the final net amount, half-up to 2 decimals.
  2. REQ-2 Payslip lines show unrounded values to 4 decimals in the PDF detail.
  3. REQ-3 The bank export total equals the sum of payslip net amounts.
  4. REQ-4 Payslips already issued are not recalculated.

Acceptance criteria

  • AC-1 A payslip with 12 allowances of 0.125 nets 1.50, not 1.56.
  • AC-2 Export of 300 payslips: total equals the sum of nets.
  • AC-3 Recalculating a closed period is refused.

Risks

Touches payroll calculation (regression risk high). Invariant in scope: “issued payslips are immutable”.
